Shift Creation Rules

  • Shifts must be between valid working hours (e.g. 06:00–22:00)
  • Staff must be available for the selected day and time
  • No overlapping shifts are allowed
  • Maximum 8 hours per day per staff member
  • Maximum 48 hours per week per staff member
  • Minimum 11 hours rest between shifts
  • Trainees must be scheduled with a Crew Trainer
  • Manager cover must exist for the full day before publishing
  • Open shifts cannot be within 24 hours of start time

What does Risk mean?

Risk indicates how suitable a staff member is for a shift. It is calculated based on working hours, rest periods, role coverage, and fairness.

Low Risk: Safe to assign
Medium Risk: Acceptable with caution
High Risk: May cause fatigue, legal, or coverage issues

Publishing

To create an open shift, click a board cell, enter the shift time, set Open Shift? to Yes, then click Create Shift. When ready, click Post Week.
